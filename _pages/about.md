I am a master's student in `Computer Science` at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/), advised by Prof. [Chenyan Xiong](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~cx/) (CMU), research scientist [Han Cai](https://han-cai.github.io/) (NVIDIA), and Prof. [Song Han](https://hanlab.mit.edu/songhan) (MIT and NVIDIA). My main research interest is the co-design of algorithms and infrastructure for efficient training and inference of large models.

I also earned my B.S. in Computer Science (`System and Network Concentration`) with an additional concentration in `Robotics` from CMU. During my undergraduate studies, I was a member of the [Catalyst Group](https://catalyst.cs.cmu.edu), advised by Prof. [Todd C. Mowry](https://www.toddcmowry.org/).

## Efficient AI

My research is dedicated to enhancing the efficiency and long-context capacity of LLMs through co-optimization of algorithms
and underlying parallel computer systems. Outside CMU, I work on efficient LLMs and am fortunate to be mentored by Prof. [Song Han](https://hanlab.mit.edu/songhan) and Research Scientist [Han Cai](https://han-cai.github.io) at MIT HAN Lab and NVIDIA.

## Robotics

Before I started doing research on LLMs, I spent 1.5 years of my undergraduate studies conducting robotics research in [DROP Lab](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/robotics-groups/drop-deep-robot-optical-perception-lab/) at CMU, where I was mentored by Prof. [Matthew Johnson-Roberson](https://computing.vanderbilt.edu/bio/matthew-johnson-roberson/) (now Dean of CCC at Vanderbilt University) and Prof. [Weiming Zhi](https://www.weimingzhi.com). I've worked with robot manipulators, quadrupeds, and humanoids, and my main focus in robotics was robotic perception for manipulation and robot foundation models.
